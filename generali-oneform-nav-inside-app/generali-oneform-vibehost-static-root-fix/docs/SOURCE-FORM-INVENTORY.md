# Source Form Inventory — blank Generali PDFs

This inventory is based on text extracted from the blank PDFs. It is not a substitute for page-by-page visual QA; where extraction order is ambiguous, use the source PDF page itself as the authority.

## PI — Professional Indemnity - Security Guards (6 pages)

### Page 1 — General Data
- Date
- Name of firm
- Business Registration No.
- When was the firm established?
- Address of Head Office
- Address of Branch Office(s), if any
- Are you licensed by Home Ministry? Yes/No
- License No. & Expiry Date
- Period of cover required: From / To
- Number of staff (permanent and temporary): Director/CEO/Principal Officer; Managers/Supervisors; Administrative/Clerical Staff; Security Guards - Armed; Security Guards - Unarmed; Others
- Applicant instructions state application must be stamped, signed and dated by Managing/Executive Director, Principal/CEO or authorized personnel/officer.

### Page 2 — Company profile and procedures
- Attach Company Profile and/or information relating to:
  - Names of Principal(s) and/or management personnel, and their experience
  - Organizational structure
  - List of major clients
- PPKKM membership Yes/No and membership reference
- Firearms equipped when on duty Yes/No; number and type
- Financial year dates From/To
- Latest audited accounts/annual report
- Turnover/gross fees/revenue: last financial year (auditor confirmed), current estimate, next financial year
- Training for guards; type and conductors
- Background checking before employment and details
- Random and ongoing drug testing
- Operating procedures reviewed periodically and frequency
- Established code of ethics for guards and details

### Page 3 — Activities and risk controls
- Breakdown of activities (%): Static guards unarmed; static guards armed; security escorts; transit cash/monetary items; overnight vaulting and bank-in service; payroll packaging; guard dogs services; CMS without patrolling; CMS with patrolling; other services
- Require clients to sign PPKKM standard service agreement? Yes/No; reasons if no
- Process of screening and conditions before employment
- How SOP is ensured before employment
- Documentary proof, licensing and procedure before hiring armed guards
- Outsource guards / use other companies' guards?
- Amount of insurance indemnity required
- Excess prepared to carry: any one claim; all claims during insurance period (the source page has multiple alternative RM lines)
- Claims made against firm in past five years; particulars

### Page 4 — Claims, previous insurance and declaration
- Partner/Principal/Director/staff disciplinary proceedings for professional misconduct; details if yes
- Claims for negligence or breach of professional duty in last ten years / circumstances notified; details if yes
- Circumstances/occurrences likely to give rise to a claim; particulars
- Previously proposed for/insured against liability; if yes: insurer name, amount of indemnity, excess borne, policy expiry date
- Insurer/company declined to insure; imposed special terms; cancelled/refused renewal
- Important Notices and declaration
- Mandatory Terms & Conditions consent
- Optional marketing consent
- Signature of Proposer
- Date
- Page 5–6: Data Privacy Notice (not application fields)

## Biz PA Proposal Form (EM) — third workflow called GPA in the agent portal (7 pages)

The official blank source title is **BIZ PA PROPOSAL FORM / BORANG CADANGAN BIZ PA**. The portal may group this under the agent's GPA workflow label, but should retain the official document title in the generated form unless Generali confirms otherwise.

### Page 1 — Proposer particulars
- Date
- Company Name
- Company Registration / Business License No.
- SST Registration No.
- Company Address
- Postcode
- Telephone No.
- Email
- Nature of Business

### Page 2 — Contacts, subsidiary and general questions
- Contact Person 1: name, designation, contact number
- Contact Person 2: name, designation, contact number
- Period of Insurance From/To
- List of subsidiaries: separate billing Yes/No; subsidiary company name, registration number and address
- General Questions 1–3 (Yes/No; full details where applicable): accident insurer defer/decline/refuse renewal/terminate; similar benefit insurance in force; claims lodged (read exact wording on page)

### Page 3 — Employer declaration, agent/broker, stamp and payment
- Employer declaration / authorized officer signature
- Agent/Broker signature
- Witnessed by
- Authorized Officer Signature
- Name
- Designation
- Company Stamp
- Dated at
- Agent name/code
- Payment Method and relevant cheque/bank/payment particulars as shown in original source

### Pages 4–6
- Data Privacy Notice in English and Bahasa Malaysia; not additional application fields.

### Page 7 — Particular of Persons to be Insured
Repeatable member table columns:
- Name
- NRIC / Passport No.
- Date of Birth
- Nationality
- Gender
- Occupation
- Occupation Class
- Plan
- Additional Benefits
- Premium (RM)

Premium summary:
- Total Premium
- 8% Service Tax
- Stamp Duty
- Total Payable

Notes in source: provide separate sheet if space is insufficient; additional benefit A = with Weekly, B = without Weekly; stamp duty/taxes subject to prevailing Malaysian law.

## Signature and stamp handling
- PI: page 1 instructions require stamped, signed and dated application; page 4 has Signature of Proposer and Date. No clearly labelled separate stamp box was detected in the blank form text; do not invent a box. Obtain insurer/agent confirmation for acceptable stamp placement.
- PL: page 3 has Signed for and on behalf of, Company Stamp, Designation. Agent verification fields are separate and must not be merged with proposer signature.
- Biz PA: page 3 includes Agent/Broker Signature, Witnessed By, Authorized Officer Signature, Name, Designation, Company Stamp, Dated at and agent/payment-related fields. Exact visual positions must be measured.
